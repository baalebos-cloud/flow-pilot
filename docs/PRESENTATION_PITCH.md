# FlowPilot presentation and pitch guide

## One-line pitch

**BMONI provides the financial rails; FlowPilot makes those rails intelligent—helping users organize, protect, and grow their money through transparent, user-approved actions.**

## Three-minute narrative

### 0:00–0:30 — Problem

“Seeing a wallet balance does not tell you what is safe to spend, what should be protected, or when idle money is losing value. Nigerian users need more than visibility; they need understandable, timely actions.”

### 0:30–1:10 — Organize

Show a CNGN 300,000 balance divided into food, transport, rent, emergency, and investment pockets. Explain that FlowPilot turns one balance into an understandable plan and protects essential allocations.

### 1:10–2:05 — Detect and explain

Show Currency Shield detecting a defined adverse movement. The recommendation identifies eligible surplus, proposes converting only a capped portion, and displays the rate, fees, evidence, downside, and remaining balances. Say clearly: “The AI explains; deterministic policy decides eligibility.”

### 2:05–2:35 — Approve and execute

The user adjusts or approves. BMONI supplies the financial infrastructure and authoritative execution state. FlowPilot records consent, prevents duplicate execution, and updates the pockets.

### 2:35–3:00 — Business value

“Every useful recommendation can increase wallet retention, engagement, and BMONI transaction/FX volume. Later, verified regulated investment partners add referral revenue without turning an untrusted model into an autonomous fund manager.”

## Trust statement

“FlowPilot never lets an LLM move money. Protected pockets are excluded, every recommendation is evidence-backed, the user sees the consequences, and execution requires explicit consent. Wallet keys and signing PINs remain on the device.”

## Revenue answer

- Increased FX conversion volume and wallet activity for BMONI.
- Higher retained balances and user engagement.
- Premium financial-planning features.
- Disclosed referral/revenue share with regulated investment partners.

## Judge questions

**Why not convert automatically?** Financial context changes. Approval protects users and produces a defensible consent trail.

**Are pockets real accounts?** In the MVP they are transparent virtual allocations unless BMONI confirms sub-wallet rails; we do not misrepresent that boundary.

**Is this investment advice?** The MVP does not execute or guarantee investments. Expansion requires verified regulated partners and formal compliance review.

**What does the AI actually do?** It explains patterns and candidate actions. Deterministic code controls eligibility, limits, protected funds, quote validity, and execution.

**How does BMONI earn?** More useful actions create FX/transaction volume and stronger wallet retention, with future partner and premium revenue paths.

## Closing line

“FlowPilot turns a passive balance into a controlled financial strategy: organize it, understand it, protect it, and grow it—with the user always in control.”
